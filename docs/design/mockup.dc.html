decision · export placement
A pane, not a modal.
1f's card becomes the right inspector's content — same 268px column, same header pattern. A modal would block the canvas for the length of a render, and renders are the one thing you want to keep working alongside. Once a render starts, the pane can be dismissed and the progress collapses into a chip in the top bar (see 2a). Clicking the chip reopens the pane.
decision · reaching the editor
Auto-open on stop. Notification only when you didn't press stop.
Pressing stop is a deliberate act and editing is what you wanted next, so the editor opens straight into the proxy-build state (2e). If the recording ended some other way — hotkey from another workspace, disk pressure, crash recovery — it lands in the list and posts a notification whose primary action is Edit. The menubar keeps a Recordings entry as the always-there path.
